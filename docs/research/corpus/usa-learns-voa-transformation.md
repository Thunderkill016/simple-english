English 1 Plus lesson 1.1 "What Are You Doing?" observed in full.
The course is built on VOA *Let's Learn English* Level 1 (Anna story)
per official OTAN/USAL docs. Lesson titles are renamed around the
language target (1.1 = present continuous), not the VOA episode title.

Legend: SE-OK = SE can reproduce the FUNCTION with legal content
(VOA assets are public domain; our own prose/images); COPY-REQ = would
require copying USAL copyrighted content (always NO → reject).

## The reusable design insight (NOT the content)

```text
FOR EACH source video episode:
  1 frame     (welcome + goals)
  2 vocab     (pre-teach → 2 retrieval checks)
  3 listen    (source asset → comprehension)
  4 language  (grammar points harvested FROM the asset →
               presentation → practice → dictation)
  5 read      (parallel leveled text on same theme →
               comprehension → noticing)
  6 speak     (pronounce forms → functional task → your-turn eval)
  7 self-eval (learning log: words + can-do)
  8 unit review lesson (retrieval across the unit)
```

This is exactly the wrapper architecture SE needs for VOA content —
and every layer is independently implementable with PD VOA assets +
original SE prose. The only thing USAL owns is their specific items,
texts, questions, and recordings. The PATTERN is unprotectable
know-how: observe → document → reimplement.

What copying would require (all REJECTED): their question items,
their leveled readings, their noticing text, their recordings,
their vocabulary images, their word lists (though USAL publishes an
"English 1 Plus vocabulary list" PDF officially for teachers —
checkable for reference only).
