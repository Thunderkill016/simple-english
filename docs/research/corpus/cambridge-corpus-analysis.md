# Cambridge Corpus Landscape — R1.1

Special research round (mission §29).

## SOURCE FACT — which corpora exist

| Corpus | Content | Access | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cambridge English Corpus (CEC) | Multi-billion-word native+learner text corpus | Internal/licensed | Proprietary |
| Cambridge Learner Corpus (CLC) | 55M+ words of real Cambridge exam scripts, 140+ L1s, 200+ countries | Licensed research corpus | Proprietary |
| Cambridge English Profile Corpus (CEPC) | Learner English built for English Profile research | Licensed | Proprietary |
| Open CLCW (public sibling) | ~1.2M-word subset of CLC released for research (via Cambridge/UCAM collaboration) | Downloadable, research license | Research-only terms — CONFIRMED existence, terms PARTIAL |
| Error-tagged corpora (FCE/CoNNL-2014 testbeds) | Public shared-task datasets derived from CLC | Downloadable | Research license |

## SOURCE FACT — how Cambridge uses them

- Coursebook decisions: Empower syllabus "informed by English Profile and the
  Cambridge English Corpus" — corpus frequency drives what appears.
- EGP was built *from* CLC: competency statements calibrated to what learners
  actually produced in exams per level.
- Dictionary examples + "common mistake" panels are corpus-derived
  (longstanding Cambridge publishing practice).
- Write&Improve/Speak&Improve trained on learner+native data (company is
  Cambridge-originated, corpus-backed).

## SOURCE FACT — public access reality

- CLC/CEC/CEPC: no public download; products only cite them.
- EGP/EVP front-ends expose *distilled* corpus findings free.
- Open research subsets exist (Open CLCW; error-tagged shared-task corpora)
  under research licenses — non-commercial, no redistribution of raw text.
- Write&Improve's underlying technology is proprietary.

## Answering the mission's questions

**What corpus-derived decisions are documented?** Syllabus level selection
(Empower), EGP level assignment, dictionary examples, common-error panels.

**What can SE actually use?**
- Open research subsets: analyzable for methodology, not redistributable.
- EGP/EVP distilled statements: reference/non-commercial.
- Nothing in this layer permits shipping Cambridge-derived text in SE.

**Can any be used commercially?** No public corpus identified so far permits
commercial use — PROHIBITED for content purposes. (Flag: verify Open CLCW's
exact license in R1.3 data-landscape round.)

## DEVIN ANALYSIS

Cambridge's moat is not the corpus files — it is the *pipeline*:
exam scripts → error-tagged learner data → level-calibrated statements →
syllabus. SE cannot copy the data but can copy the *idea*: a learner-error
layer (what real beginners actually produce) should inform leveling. The
public substitute for CLC-scale evidence does not exist; honest fallback =
EGP/EVP statements + open learner corpora if any are found in R1.3.
