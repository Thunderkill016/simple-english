## Lesson 1A — "say your name and country" (CONFIRMED)

- GETTING STARTED: picture-based lead-in.
- 1 READING AND GRAMMAR
  - a. audio: listen, then say "Hi, I'm …" with own name
  - b. audio+text: read & listen to 3 short intro conversations; match
    conversations to picture groups (Vilma/Camila; Harumi/Katy/Paul; Ruben/Li)
  - c. complete be-table (positive + questions): I am / you are / we are
  - d. → Grammar Focus 1A Part 1 (p.114)
  - e. Sound & spelling: long vs short sounds — listen (I'm, We're, from, the…),
    notice, practise
  - f. practise the 3 conversations: pairs → groups of three → new partner
  - g. practise again **using own name**
- Vocabulary + further listening + speaking sections (PARTIAL — countries set
  and a listening confirmed; exercise count beyond §1b not fully visible).

Observable depth (1A visible portion): ≥ 7 exercise items, ≥ 2 audio items,
3 speaking-practice passes, 1 pronunciation micro-focus, 1 grammar focus.

## Lesson 1C — "meet and greet new people" (CONFIRMED — fullest reproduction)

Story: Sophia's first day at Electric Blue Technology (3-part video).

- 1 LISTENING (Part 1): predict from picture → watch 1.25 and check →
  watch again and complete sentences (choose correct word, e.g. meet/see;
  home/office; desk/chair).
- 2 USEFUL LANGUAGE — Greeting people:
  - a. complete time bands → evening/morning/afternoon
  - b. listen 1.26: syllable count in greetings (hi=1; good|mor|ning…)
  - c. listen again: underline stressed syllables
  - d. match times to correct expression, with partner
  - e. 1.30 sort response expressions into a table ("I'm fine, thanks." /
    "I'm OK, thank you." / "Oh, not bad, thanks." / "I'm good, thank you.")
  - f. re-practise conversation changing roles + new expressions
- 3 LISTENING + USEFUL LANGUAGE (Part 2): who is the manager? (option MC) →
  listen 1.28 underline words heard → 1.29 main-stress pronunciation →
  practise conversation with partner.
- 4 LISTENING + USEFUL LANGUAGE (Part 3): are Sophia and Megan friends? →
  1.31 underline correct answers → 1.32 order the conversation, listen+check →
  groups of three practise **with own names**.
- 5 PRONUNCIATION — Tone: 1.33 does the tone change or stay the same (×5
  phrases) → listen again + repeat.
- 6 SPEAKING: 1.34 complete the conversation, listen+check → groups of three
  practise with own names.
- 7 WRITING: read Sophia's profile → Writing Plus 1C (capital letters, full
  stops) → write own class profile with frames ("Hi/Hello, my … I'm from … in
  … I'm in a class with … in room …") → read others' profiles.
- CHECK YOUR PROGRESS → "You can now do the Unit Progress Test."

Observable depth (1C alone): ~24 exercise items, 7+ audio/video items
(1.25–1.34 + 3 video parts), 4 explicit speaking-practice blocks,
3 pronunciation focuses, 1 guided writing task, 1 self-check + external test.

## DEVIN ANALYSIS — what this says about "one lesson"

A Cambridge first-unit meet/greet lesson is ~25 exercise items deep, wraps
one narrative input in 3 passes, and never ends without (a) production in own
words, (b) a written product, (c) self-check, (d) external progress test.
The current SE "lesson" (1 audio + 1 MC + 1 production frame) is roughly the
size of *one exercise block* inside 1C — i.e., ~1/20 of the depth envelope.
